## Rights/provenance evidence and limitations

Copyright infringement is prohibited by [Google's Publisher Policies](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10502938?hl=en). Lack of a license record is not by itself proof of infringement. The [US Copyright Office distinguishes unprotected facts/methods from protectable expression](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html). Dataset selection/arrangement, site terms, database rights, jurisdiction and permission history may require separate assessment. Attribution is different from permission. These findings therefore identify supported sources and remaining review tasks, not legal clearance conclusions.
