# Finflo quality follow-up — November 9, 2026

The request for every category to reach 10/10 was treated as an instruction to improve actual quality. Scores have not been inflated. This follow-up adds corrections and evidence to the [initial audit](adsense-audit-2026-11-09.md); it does not certify Google's approval or replace missing account, ownership, rights or field-performance evidence.
